Drawbacks of Waterfall Model in Software Engineering
The Waterfall model is a traditional Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) model that follows a sequential approach. While simple, it has several limitations.

🔍 Key Drawbacks
Rigid and inflexible structure
Difficult to handle changing requirements
Testing happens very late
High risk and uncertainty
Limited customer involvement
No early working software
Not suitable for large or complex projects
❌ Why Waterfall Often Fails
Requirements are assumed to be fixed
Errors discovered late are costly
Customer feedback is delayed
No iteration or improvement during development
📊 Waterfall vs Agile
Feature	Waterfall	Agile
Flexibility	Low	High
Customer Feedback	Late	Continuous
Testing	After development	Continuous
Delivery	End of project	Incremental
📌 Conclusion
Due to its rigidity and lack of adaptability, the Waterfall model is rarely used in modern software development where requirements change frequently.
